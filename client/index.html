Quick bet

Each winning payline pays 1× the line bet for 3 matching symbols, 2× for 4, or 3× for 5 from left to right. Each complete row or column in the 3×3 bonus puzzle returns one line bet.

Fortune Wheel additionally triggers on 1 in 20 spins. Its six equally likely awards are 1, 2, 3, 5, 8 or 10 times the line bet; the server selects and settles the award.

Classic Reels is an alternate presentation of Lucky Slots, with the same rules and payouts, not a separate game.

3D Slots uses Babylon.js WebGL presentation. Its symbols and awards are selected by the server, using the same rules and payouts.

Demo only. Credits have no cash value.
